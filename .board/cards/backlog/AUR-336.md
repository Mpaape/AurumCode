## Outcome

Um manifesto lexical registra hash, targets, dependency names, shell-feature
classes, consumers e disposição do `Makefile`, provando `executed=false` e
preservando seus bytes.

## Non-goals

- Executar `make`, expandir recipe/variable, instalar tool ou corrigir targets.
- Inventariar ignore files, changelog ou instruções GEMINI, separados em AUR-355..357.

## Preconditions

- AUR-001 sela o Makefile e AUR-009 fornece canário/process sinks.
- Parser lexical opera em arquivo regular bounded, sem Make ou shell subprocess.

## Postconditions

- Manifesto contém um row do Makefile, target/dependency digests, disposition e `executed=false`.
- Source hash e process table audit permanecem limpos no replay.

## Acceptance scenarios

### AC-001: Manifestar Makefile por análise lexical

- Given: Makefile hash-lockado e fixture com recipe hostil/canário sintético.
- When: parser enumera targets e classes sem avaliar variáveis ou recipes.
- Then: manifesto completo registra `executed=false` e nenhum subprocesso/output de recipe.
- And: bytes e digest permanecem idênticos.

- Covers requirements: [PR-ARCH-001, PR-EVD-002]
- Covers controls: [CR-EVD-001, CR-EVD-002, CR-EVD-003, CR-REV-001, CR-REV-002, CR-REV-003, CR-REV-004, CR-REV-005, CR-GATE-001, CR-TRUST-001, CR-TRUST-004]

## Skeptical mutations

### MUT-001: AC-001 / repository

- Change: alterar target na fixture depois do hash lock.
- Expected: source/manifest digest diverge e AC-001 falha.

### MUT-002: AC-001 / build-tool

- Change: executar recipe sintética durante discovery.
- Expected: process/canary gates invalidam `executed=false` e vetam o resultado.

## Security and privacy

- Data flow: tokens/classes/digests -> TSV; recipes não chegam a shell/log/evidence.
- Secrets: canário sintético em recipe é ausente dos sinks.
- Least privilege: read-only, zero subprocess/network/tool installation.

## Documentation

- `docs/specs/AUR-336.md` registra targets/classes/consumers e disposition.

## Compatibility, migration, rollback

- Compatibility: Makefile permanece byte-idêntico e não endossado.
- Migration: cada target mantido recebe replacement/test próprio.
- Rollback: remover somente derivados.

## Review

- Reviewer: one independent reviewer reads the exact immutable candidate in a clean detached worktree and checks every changed hunk, the public contract, paths/read_paths, acceptance, declared selectors, mutations, profile/runtime compatibility, security boundaries, and exit-code handling.
- Independence: the reviewer is separate from the builder and does not edit the candidate or write evidence in the coordinator checkout. No extra review role is required.
- Verdict: approve only on raw exit 0 with real assertions. Runtime, loader, image, dependency, or other infrastructure failures are blocked or inconclusive, never approval. The validator reruns the declared acceptance on the same SHA when validation is not none.

## Evidence

Only the coordinator writes sanitized evidence under .board/evidence/<card-id>/; tests and builders emit observations only. Evidence binds the exact candidate SHA, review verdict, acceptance/validation exits, mutation result when declared, clean tree, and artifact hashes. Never store secrets, raw prompts/responses, credentials, environment values, or chain-of-thought.
