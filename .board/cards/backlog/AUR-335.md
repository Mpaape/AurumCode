## Outcome

Um manifesto sanitizado compara chaves, tipos, default classes e incompatibilidades
de `configs/default-config.yml` e `configs/.aurumcode/config.example.yml` ao
schema v3, sem carregar valores no domínio ou ambiente.

## Non-goals

- Resolver credenciais, aplicar config, editar YAML ou migrar chaves neste card.
- Caracterizar exemplos `.env`, isolados em AUR-358.

## Preconditions

- AUR-001 sela os dois hashes, AUR-009 fornece canário e AUR-030 define schema v3.
- Parser rejeita duplicate keys, aliases cíclicos e tipos desconhecidos antes do manifesto.

## Postconditions

- Ambos paths têm key-set/type/default-class digest, incompatibilidades e `secret_values=0`.
- Arquivos e ambiente do processo permanecem idênticos.

## Acceptance scenarios

### AC-001: Comparar YAML sem importar valores

- Given: dois arquivos hash-lockados e fixtures com duplicate key, tipo inválido e canário.
- When: parser estrito extrai somente nomes/classes e compara ao schema AUR-030.
- Then: ambos aparecem, cada incompatibilidade tem disposição e nenhum valor entra no output.
- And: replay e env snapshot são idênticos.

- Covers requirements: [PR-ARCH-001, PR-CFG-002, PR-EVD-002]
- Covers controls: [CR-EVD-001, CR-EVD-002, CR-EVD-003, CR-REV-001, CR-REV-002, CR-REV-003, CR-REV-004, CR-REV-005, CR-GATE-001, CR-TRUST-001, CR-TRUST-004]

## Public contract

- `manifest.tsv` expõe path, schema/key/type/default digests, incompatibilidades, disposition e secret count; nunca YAML values.

## Skeptical mutations

### MUT-001: AC-001 / repository

- Change: omitir um dos YAML do manifesto mantendo-o no snapshot.
- Expected: source/manifest coverage diverge e AC-001 falha.

### MUT-002: AC-001 / configuration

- Change: aceitar duplicate key ou tipo incompatível como válido.
- Expected: schema mutation é detectada antes da persistência.

### MUT-003: AC-001 / secret-channel

- Change: inserir canário credential-shaped em fixture e persistir o value.
- Expected: secret count/sink gates vetam sem revelar o canário.

## Security and privacy

- Data flow: YAML -> nomes/classes/digests -> TSV; values/env são proibidos.
- Secrets: somente canário sintético; zero resolution ou output de value.
- Least privilege: read-only, zero provider/network/env mutation.

## Documentation

- `docs/specs/AUR-335.md` registra incompatibilidades e dispositions para migração.

## Compatibility, migration, rollback

- Compatibility: apenas caracteriza; defaults existentes não viram contrato v3.
- Migration: cada chave aprovada recebe dry-run posterior.
- Rollback: remover derivados mantendo YAML intacto.

## Review

- Reviewer: one independent reviewer reads the exact immutable candidate in a clean detached worktree and checks every changed hunk, the public contract, paths/read_paths, acceptance, declared selectors, mutations, profile/runtime compatibility, security boundaries, and exit-code handling.
- Independence: the reviewer is separate from the builder and does not edit the candidate or write evidence in the coordinator checkout. No extra review role is required.
- Verdict: approve only on raw exit 0 with real assertions. Runtime, loader, image, dependency, or other infrastructure failures are blocked or inconclusive, never approval. The validator reruns the declared acceptance on the same SHA when validation is not none.

## Evidence

Only the coordinator writes sanitized evidence under .board/evidence/<card-id>/; tests and builders emit observations only. Evidence binds the exact candidate SHA, review verdict, acceptance/validation exits, mutation result when declared, clean tree, and artifact hashes. Never store secrets, raw prompts/responses, credentials, environment values, or chain-of-thought.
