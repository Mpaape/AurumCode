## Review

- Reviewer: one independent reviewer reads the exact immutable candidate in a clean detached worktree and checks every changed hunk, the public contract, paths/read_paths, acceptance, declared selectors, mutations, profile/runtime compatibility, security boundaries, and exit-code handling.
- Independence: the reviewer is separate from the builder and does not edit the candidate or write evidence in the coordinator checkout. No extra review role is required.
- Verdict: approve only on raw exit 0 with real assertions. Runtime, loader, image, dependency, or other infrastructure failures are blocked or inconclusive, never approval. The validator reruns the declared acceptance on the same SHA when validation is not none.

## Evidence

Only the coordinator writes sanitized evidence under .board/evidence/<card-id>/; tests and builders emit observations only. Evidence binds the exact candidate SHA, review verdict, acceptance/validation exits, mutation result when declared, clean tree, and artifact hashes. Never store secrets, raw prompts/responses, credentials, environment values, or chain-of-thought.

## Dispatch block

- 2026-08-12: returned to backlog after four builder rounds and four independent
  reviews, under the rule that two reviews without a delivery end the approach.
  The candidate is preserved at `8b9ad928fa69a09911e29ff0ca8eddeaf8949a09`
  (tag `cand/AUR-013-mecanismo-aprovado`); do not rebuild from zero.
- What is settled: the selection mechanism was approved in the third review and
  survived every attack in the fourth. The allowlist is loaded independently of
  the record being audited, the verified artifact is bound to the candidate, and
  seventeen hostile vectors, both declared mutations and both historical attacks
  all fail closed with behavioural codes.
- What blocks it: the card must document what it audits, and every round the
  documentation-gating layer added to satisfy the previous finding introduced a
  new claim wider than the check behind it. Round four proved two: the
  documentation gate only verifies that the run is contained in the document and
  never the reverse, so false plugin claims can be added silently; and the
  documented example is validated line by line rather than as a whole vector, so
  one line can be swapped for another vector's line.
- Root cause to fix before any redispatch, at the card level and not by another
  builder attempt: this card must audit an artifact that its own `paths` cannot
  materialize inside the container, so its documentation necessarily asserts more
  than its acceptance can observe. Either give the card the skill roots it audits
  in `read_paths` so the claims become checkable, or narrow the Outcome to what
  the sealed vectors alone can prove and drop the documentation-gating layer.
