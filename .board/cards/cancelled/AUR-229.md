## Outcome

o worker nega rede, socket do engine, host mounts, devices e namespaces compartilhados antes da execução.

## Non-goals

- Definir o usuário não-root, o rootfs read-only e os limites de CPU, memória e PIDs: este card cobre somente as superfícies de rede e de host.
- Publicação e credenciais, salvo quando explicitamente nomeadas no outcome.

## Preconditions

- os manifests aprovados de AUR-227 estão disponíveis na mesma base.
- Acceptance selado, árvore limpa e CandidateIdentityV1 completa.

## Postconditions

- o worker nega rede, socket do engine, host mounts, devices e namespaces compartilhados antes da execução.
- Nenhum arquivo fora de `paths` ou capability não declarada muda.

## Acceptance scenarios

### AC-001: Negar egress e superfícies do host

- Given: o manifest aprovado de AUR-227 fixa o selector de engine na mesma base, e `tests/containers/sandbox/isolation` traz probes que tentam DNS, conexão TCP de saída e acesso ao socket do engine.
- When: o perfil de `internal/sandbox/profile/isolation.go` monta o argv e o worker executa cada probe de rede, socket do engine, host mount, device e namespace compartilhado.
- Then: o argv não contém rede diferente de none, bind do socket do engine, host mount, device nem namespace compartilhado, e todo probe de egress falha antes de estabelecer conexão.
- And: replay da mesma CandidateIdentityV1 é estruturalmente idêntico.

## Review

- Reviewer: one independent reviewer reads the exact immutable candidate in a clean detached worktree and checks every changed hunk, the public contract, paths/read_paths, acceptance, declared selectors, mutations, profile/runtime compatibility, security boundaries, and exit-code handling.
- Independence: the reviewer is separate from the builder and does not edit the candidate or write evidence in the coordinator checkout. No extra review role is required.
- Verdict: approve only on raw exit 0 with real assertions. Runtime, loader, image, dependency, or other infrastructure failures are blocked or inconclusive, never approval. The validator reruns the declared acceptance on the same SHA when validation is not none.

## Evidence

Only the coordinator writes sanitized evidence under .board/evidence/<card-id>/; tests and builders emit observations only. Evidence binds the exact candidate SHA, review verdict, acceptance/validation exits, mutation result when declared, clean tree, and artifact hashes. Never store secrets, raw prompts/responses, credentials, environment values, or chain-of-thought.
