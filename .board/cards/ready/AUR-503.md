## Outcome

Os ramos de command injection entregues pelo AUR-486 nao acusam mais uma
Mencao textual: `exec.Command`, `Process.Start`, `Invoke-Expression`/`iex` e
`eval` dentro de comentario ou string literal deixam de gerar achado, e
`eval :=`/`eval =` (declaracao) tambem, enquanto a invocacao real continua
sendo encontrada.

## Achado medido (2026-09-15)

O revisor do AUR-486 reproduziu, nos ramos novos, o mesmo falso positivo de
classe que a rodada 2 corrigiu so em `sh -c`/SQL: `// exec.Command("sh","-c",…)`,
`// Process.Start("cmd.exe","/c ping "+host)`, `# Invoke-Expression …`,
`Write-Host "Invoke-Expression is unsafe"`, `# iex is dangerous` e
`eval := compute()` produzem achado. O AUR-462 fixou a regra: falso positivo em
portao de merge ensina o time a desligar o portao.

## Non-goals

- Nao introduzir AST nem analise semantica.
- Nao cobrir toda forma de comentario aninhado/heredoc; o que nao for provado
  fica declarado no spec como limitacao conhecida.
- Nao afrouxar as deteccoes reais.

- AC-003: `eval := ...` / `eval = ...` nao produzem achado; `eval "ping $1"` /
  `eval $cmd` produzem.

## Review

- Reviewer: one independent reviewer reads the exact immutable candidate in a clean detached worktree, runs the declared command, and checks every changed hunk, the public contract, paths/read_paths, acceptance, declared selectors, mutations, profile/runtime compatibility, security boundaries, and exit-code handling.
- Independence: the reviewer is separate from the builder and does not edit the candidate or write evidence in the coordinator checkout. No extra review role is required.
