# AUR-233 - Verify the bootstrap lockset

## Outcome

`BootstrapLocksetV1` accepts exactly AUR-359 through AUR-364 when every locked
artifact exists, its content digest matches the index, and the aggregate
identity recomputes from the sorted child/digest pairs.

## Contract

- The index is `.board/bootstrap/locks.yml`.
- The index contains one `schema`, one aggregate `identity`, and exactly six
  `child` rows.
- Each child is bound to its embedded artifact path; aliases are rejected.
- Digests are recomputed from artifact bytes. A value written in the index is
  not trusted merely because it has the right shape.
- The aggregate identity is `sha256:` plus the SHA-256 digest of sorted lines
  in the form `<card-id> <recomputed-digest>`.
- Verdict words and hand-written outcome claims are not accepted in the index.
- The index is non-empty and at most 64 KiB/512 lines; each artifact is
  non-empty and at most 4 MiB.

Typed failures are `lock_domain_missing`, `child_duplicate`,
`child_digest_mismatch`, and `candidate_identity_mismatch`. Child execution
failures are reported separately by the acceptance harness and are never
converted into a successful aggregate.

## Validation

`tests/acceptance/AUR-233.sh` exercises the nominal domain, missing and
duplicate children, digest and identity tampering, self-reference, handwritten
verdicts, aliasing, malformed index lines, symlink artifacts, and child
re-execution. `tests/gates/bootstrap/AUR-233/cases.tsv` is the compact mutation
matrix. For example, deleting the AUR-363 row returns
`lock_domain_missing`, while changing one artifact's content returns
`child_digest_mismatch`. `bash .board/bootstrap/verify.sh` performs the same
deterministic index, bounds, artifact, and identity checks without executing a
child.

## Non-goals

This card does not produce or reinterpret child artifacts, install toolchains,
resolve credentials, or publish external state. Child acceptance programs are
re-executed by the acceptance harness and their raw exit status remains the
source of the child observation.
